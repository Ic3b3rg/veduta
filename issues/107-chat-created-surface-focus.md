# 107 — Reveal a Surface created by the initiating chat turn

> _This was generated by AI during triage._

## Problem

A successful Surface creation from chat can produce no perceptible change in Home. The Agent reports
success, the Gateway persists and broadcasts the Surface, and the PWA renders its card, but the new
card can be appended below the viewport with no scroll or highlight. The user is left unable to tell
whether the requested action changed the GenUI.

This affects Template instantiation through `create_surface_from_template` and justified direct
creation through `create_surface`. A rejected direct creation correctly creates nothing and must
remain quiet.

## Reproduction

1. Focus a Space whose Surface list extends below the viewport.
2. Ask the Agent to instantiate a Template as a new Surface, or to perform an allowed direct Surface
   creation.
3. Wait for the Agent to report successful tool completion.
4. Observe that Home remains at the same scroll position and does not visibly identify the created
   Surface.

The reporter reproduced this with the Groceries Template as Weekly groceries, followed by a
justified direct copy named Direct groceries.

## Verified behavior

A real-browser reproduction against the local Gateway confirmed all of the following after the
success message:

- the Space Surface count increased;
- the persisted snapshot contained the new schema-valid Surface;
- the new Surface card existed in the DOM;
- the card began at vertical position 1300 in an 800-pixel viewport;
- the page remained at scroll position 0;
- the card was outside the viewport and had no creation highlight.

This isolates the defect to initiating-tab presentation rather than creation, persistence, event
delivery, or rendering.

## Scope boundary

This issue owns local feedback for a Surface created by the initiating chat turn. Gateway-owned
Pinned and regular ordering remains in #106. The general new-activity indicator remains in #68.
Template matching, provenance, and the direct-create justification gate remain unchanged.
