# 096 — Define one channel-neutral Pending-decision contract

> _This was generated by AI during triage._

## Verified gap

Veduta already has several daemon-owned workflows that wait for an explicit user choice: trust
Approval cards, Tree proposals, Space proposals, and verified update offers. Each workflow owns its
state, Surface actions, and lifecycle independently. There is no shared representation through
which another authenticated user channel can list or resolve the same decision by stable identity.

This fragmentation is now user-visible. A notification can refer to an Approval Surface without
being able to resolve or open it, chat cannot truthfully act on a pending choice, and each new
decision workflow would otherwise need another parallel channel-specific path.

## Desired direction

Introduce **Pending decision** as the channel-neutral daemon contract for an explicit user choice.
Every decision has a durable identity, kind, safe summary, ownership, allowed resolutions, state,
and truthful outcome. Its validated Surface, fixed-shell notification, and chat affordance are
different channels to that same decision; none creates a second action or lets model prose stand in
for a recorded user choice.

Adapt every current daemon-owned decision workflow to the contract while preserving its existing
authority and invariants. Approval decisions still resolve through the trust layer, Tree proposals
still re-check their target version, Space proposals still create exactly once, and update offers
still require a verified release. Future decision kinds should join through the same contract.

## Acceptance boundary

- Every current daemon-owned decision can be listed through one validated, safe contract with a
  stable id, kind, scope/ownership, allowed resolutions, current state, and optional Decision
  Surface identity.
- Resolving by exact id validates that the requested resolution is allowed, records
  `trusted:user` as the actor, and delegates to the owning workflow rather than reproducing its
  business logic.
- Competing or repeated resolutions produce at most one state transition and at most one external
  effect; callers receive the already-terminal state instead of replaying work.
- Untrusted content, proactive work, provider-native tools, and model-authored assertions cannot
  manufacture a user decision.
- Restart recovery and reconnect expose the same pending/terminal truth without resurrecting or
  duplicating a resolved decision.
- Existing trust, provenance, audit, Event log, Surface validation, update verification, and Tree
  proposal stale-target guarantees remain intact.

## Out of scope

- Natural-language selection and disambiguation in chat.
- Fixed-shell presentation, navigation, or outcome notifications.
- Replacing the workflow-specific Decision Surfaces or allowing provider-native approvals.
