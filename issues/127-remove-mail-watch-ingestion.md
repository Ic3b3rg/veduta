# 127 — Remove superseded Gmail Watch and IMAP IDLE ingestion paths

> _This was generated by AI during triage._

## What to build

Contract the personal-mail migration after native Gmail and Skill-driven Himalaya Mailbox connections
can adopt every supported legacy configuration. Remove the dormant Gmail-mail Watch, Pub/Sub
history-ingestion, IMAP IDLE, mail-specific renewal/health wiring, and the imapflow runtime
dependency. Keep reusable Gmail OAuth/API primitives for the native connector, keep migrated
Himalaya account material available to its Skill, and leave Google Calendar Watch plus generic HMAC
webhook ingestion unchanged.

Retain the minimum versioned data migration needed for an older installation to become passive on
its first upgraded boot. Once migrated, obsolete topic, subscription, cursor, UID, and mail-source
state must no longer participate in runtime behavior or appear as active settings. Do not delete
mailbox credentials, passive connections, Space data, Events, or unrelated ingestion state.

Align architecture, security, onboarding, issue specifications, and historical decisions. Closed
issues #12 and #25 remain historical records, with explicit supersession links rather than rewritten
claims about what originally shipped; ADR-0023 remains in the repository marked superseded by
ADR-0024.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No production module starts, renews, reconnects, catches up, queues, or reports health for a
      Gmail-mail Watch or IMAP IDLE source.
- [ ] imapflow and mail-only ingestion configuration, routes, timers, cursors, alerts, tests, and
      active documentation are absent after the contract step.
- [ ] Gmail on-demand search/open/reply and direct Himalaya Skill behavior remain unchanged and use
      only passive Mailbox connections.
- [ ] Google Calendar Watch still renews and fetches events, and generic HMAC webhooks retain their
      authentication, filtering, quarantine, queue, and health behavior.
- [ ] An installation upgrading directly from the pre-Mailbox schema migrates idempotently without
      provider contact, credential loss, boot failure, or re-creating obsolete state on a later
      restart.
- [ ] Migration cleanup never deletes passive connections, vault entries still in use, Spaces,
      Surfaces, Events, Automations, or unrelated source configuration.
- [ ] ADR-0023, ADR-0024, issues #12/#25, architecture, security, onboarding, and current
      specifications tell one non-contradictory historical and active story.
- [ ] Dependency removal, clean install, direct upgrade, repeated upgrade, Calendar/webhook
      regression, Gmail/Himalaya regression, and pnpm check pass.

## Out of scope

- Removing Google Calendar Watch or generic webhook ingestion.
- Adding another mail provider or capability.
- Rewriting or deleting historical ADRs and closed issue discussions.
