The first Hub release proof requires **Gog, Obsidian, and Brave**, not a toy package. Self-improving
Agent and Hermes Web Search Plus remain hard compatibility cases with their own issues; neither is
counted unless its complete required behavior passes. WhatsApp remains a separate Bridge decision
under [ADR-0028](../adr/0028-concrete-bridge-extension-seam.md), even though its ClawHub plugin
ranked #1 in the same snapshot. Live disposable proofs belong to issues #184–#188 and #193; this
decision records the exact failures and gates to exercise, not a claim that they passed.
