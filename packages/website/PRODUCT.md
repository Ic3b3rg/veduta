## Users

Visitors evaluating Veduta and potential alpha testers. This distribution surface helps them
understand the product before choosing a local trial or self-hosting.

## Product Purpose

Show a Space, a useful Surface, a Chat-driven change and a retained result. Lead visitors to
GitHub for source, installation, current limitations and contributions.

owns recording and publication instructions. Public hosting and its canonical URL are undecided.
